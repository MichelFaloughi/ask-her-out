Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything you need to ask her out is free, with no account and no limits. Animated themes and the stats card are optional paid extras.

What does she see?

A page with your photo, your question, the plan and your note, plus a Yes button and a No button that runs away from her cursor. Try the preview on the right.

Is my photo private?

Only people with the link can see it. Links are unguessable, never listed anywhere, and each invite is deleted after a year.

Will she know I used this?

There's a small "Sent via ask-her-out.com" line at the bottom of her page. The Premium Pass removes it.

Can I edit an invite after creating it?

Not yet. Creating a new one takes thirty seconds, so make a fresh one and send the new link.
